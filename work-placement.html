Prepare to learn safely at work

Work placement is a real workplace learning experience. This route prepares conduct, WHS, communication, privacy, feedback and reflection while leaving provider administration with the school.

Open placement checklistOpen reflection record
1

Professional conduct

Confirm arrival, presentation, phone and break expectations before the placement. Be punctual, honest about your training and ready to ask when a task is unclear.

WHS and induction

Attend the workplace induction, learn emergency and reporting routes, follow training and use equipment only within authorisation. A placement workplace can differ from the school training environment.

Communication

Listen, repeat critical details, clarify unfamiliar terms and close the loop on tasks. Tell the supervisor early if a change, delay or mistake affects another person.

Absence or lateness

Use only the confirmed school and provider process. Record the correct contacts before the first day; do not rely on a classmate to pass on a message.

Privacy and media

Protect customer, staff and business information. Do not photograph people, records, screens, recipes or work areas unless the school and workplace have explicitly authorised the purpose and handling.

Goal and feedback

Set one observable learning goal, request feedback at an appropriate time and convert it into a specific next action rather than a vague promise to improve.

Daily reflection

Record task context, your action, feedback, observed result and next step without copying confidential workplace information or claiming an assessor judgement.

Before the first day

Your teacher will confirm: provider, dates, contacts, absence and emergency routes, travel, insurance information, official hours record and submission process.

Student check

  • I know who to contact and how.
  • I know arrival and presentation expectations.
  • I know what information and images must stay private.
  • I have one realistic learning goal.

Ask at induction

  • What are the emergency and first-aid arrangements?
  • How are hazards, incidents and equipment faults reported?
  • Which tasks and equipment are within my role and training?
  • Who checks my work and gives feedback?
