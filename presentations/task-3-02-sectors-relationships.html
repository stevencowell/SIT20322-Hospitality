Worked example

A sector groups related functions, not identical businesses

Industry sectors help organise businesses with related main functions. Accommodation businesses provide places to stay and connected guest services. Food and beverage businesses prepare, sell or serve food and drinks in varied settings. Functions, venues, clubs and visitor services can overlap with these areas, while each business still has its own service style, customers, size and operating context.

A hotel may connect accommodation, reception, housekeeping, food and beverage, functions and maintenance. A small motel may use a much narrower role structure. Both are accommodation contexts, but their work relationships are not identical.

Question the room

Why is a supplier part of a hospitality service system?

  1. Because supplier information, availability, quality or timing can affect what the venue can promise and deliver.
  2. Because suppliers make every customer-service decision for the venue.
  3. Because only managers ever use supplier information.
