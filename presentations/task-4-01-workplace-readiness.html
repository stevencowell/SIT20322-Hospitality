Task 4 · Lesson 1

Workplace readiness: prepare the whole service system

Plan and prepare for a service period by confirming current information, understanding the role, checking the work area and communicating problems before they affect customers or the team.

Learning intention

What success looks like

  • I can explain how preparation, service and end-of-service duties form one connected system.
  • I can identify the current information needed before work begins and clarify anything uncertain.
  • I can separate my own responsibilities from shared team responsibilities.
  • I can describe a source-safe pre-service check without inventing local procedures or equipment settings.

Worked example

A service period is a connected system

Hospitality performance is not a set of isolated product skills. A service period connects preparation, customer interaction, operational work and end-of-service duties. A clean cup, an accurate order and a well-made beverage still depend on the station being ready, the correct information reaching the right people and the final product being checked before handover.

A milk option is unavailable before service. Reporting it during preparation allows the menu information, order-taking team and beverage station to use the same message. Discovering it after several orders have been accepted creates rework and avoidable customer disappointment.

Question the room

The printed beverage list and the supervisor's current briefing appear to disagree. What is the strongest first action?

  1. Use whichever source gives the easiest preparation method.
  2. Pause and clarify the current instruction with the responsible person before accepting or preparing affected orders.
  3. Keep both versions in use and let each worker choose.

The espresso machine has just shown an unfamiliar warning. No drink has been started. The supervisor needs to be told before equipment use continues.

  1. Build a concise handover from the stimulus.
  2. Deliver it to a partner.
  3. Receiver repeats the order, constraint and next action.
