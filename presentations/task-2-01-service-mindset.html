  • Distinguish an external customer interaction from service to an internal customer or colleague.

Worked example

More than being friendly

Customer service is the interaction and support a business provides before, during and after a customer receives a product or service. In hospitality, the experience begins before the first order: the venue is presented, information is available and the customer is acknowledged. It continues through questions, ordering, production, delivery, checking satisfaction and responding if something changes.

A customer phones to ask whether a table is available, arrives, orders, receives a delay update, eats and later gives feedback. Every contact is part of the same service experience.

Question the room

Which example treats customer service as a complete experience?

  1. Smile when the customer arrives, then avoid checking whether the order is accurate.
  2. Prepare, welcome, clarify, record, deliver, check satisfaction and use feedback.
  3. Deliver the product quickly even when an important request is unclear.

Speaker notes and delivery prompts
  1. Open with this purpose: Build a service mindset by tracing what customers and colleagues experience before, during and after a hospitality transaction.
  2. Use More than being friendly to connect prior learning to the first observable workplace decision.
  3. Model More than being friendly by walking through this supplied example: A customer phones to ask whether a table is available, arrives, orders, receives a delay update, eats and later gives feedback. Every contact is part of the same service experience.
  4. Ask: Which example treats customer service as a complete experience? Confirm the strongest response as Prepare, welcome, clarify, record, deliver, check satisfaction and use feedback..
  5. Listen for this misconception: A greeting is only one moment. Without accurate delivery and follow-up, the service cycle is incomplete.
