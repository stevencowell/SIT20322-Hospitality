Worked example

Know the beverage family before choosing the method

Non-alcoholic beverage work can include teas, non-espresso coffees, juices, smoothies, milk-based drinks, mixed cold beverages and other products on the authorised menu. Espresso beverages are handled through the separate espresso workflow because they require a commercial espresso machine and grinder.

Two drinks may both be served cold but require different methods and equipment. A blended beverage and a shaken beverage cannot be treated as interchangeable just because the finished colour looks similar.

Question the room

A customer asks for something cold, not too sweet and without espresso. What is the best first response?

  1. Choose the most popular cold drink and begin making it.
  2. Ask a broad question about preferred flavour or style, then clarify relevant authorised options and ingredient needs.
  3. Explain that all cold drinks are similar once ice is added.

Apply

Beverage and serviceware match

The venue serves water, chilled juice, tea and teacher-directed espresso beverages. Exact sizes and local presentation standards come from the authorised service procedure.

  1. Match four service needs.
  2. Check the handling and suitability explanation.
  3. Write a five-point pre-service serviceware check.
