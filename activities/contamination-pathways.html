Break the contamination pathway

Match contamination events to the most immediate pathway-breaking control.

Supplied context

The scenario

During sandwich preparation, several objects move between raw-food, cleaning and ready-to-eat zones.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Select the most immediate control for each event.
  2. Check and correct the six pathways.
  3. Choose one event and trace source → route → food or person at risk → control.

Success criteria

  • Transfer route identified
  • Control acts before exposure continues
  • Gloves are not treated as a handwashing substitute

Support

Name what became contaminated before choosing the next action.

Extension

Add a monitoring check that would show whether the corrected control stayed effective.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
