Receiving and storage decision trail

Use supplied acceptance evidence to accept, isolate, reject or escalate a food delivery without inventing a check.

Supplied context

The scenario

The teacher has nominated the current acceptance procedure. Work only from the evidence given in each card and the authorised source linked on the page.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

A sealed chilled item is within the authorised acceptance range, packaging is intact and identification is clear.

A potentially hazardous delivery does not meet the current acceptance criterion.

Packaging is torn and contamination may have occurred.

No temperature was measured, but the record has a blank box.

Method

Steps

  1. Read the four evidence cards.
  2. Choose an action and read the feedback.
  3. Complete one monitor → failure evidence → corrective action → record chain.

Success criteria

  • Actions use supplied evidence
  • Failed checks stay out of use
  • No measurements are invented
  • Notification and record follow the action

Support

Use the chain: check → compare → protect → notify → correct → record.

Extension

Explain why stock rotation cannot make an unsafe delivery acceptable.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
