Task 2 · Activity 12

Booking and order accuracy check

Identify missing or ambiguous details before a booking or order is passed to another work area.

30 minutesIndividual, then role-playAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

Incoming call: 'Sam Lee, table for four next Friday at six. One guest needs step-free access. Please call me on 0400 555 018 if there is a problem.' Draft record: Name—Sam; People—4; Time—6; Note—access; Contact—blank.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Compare the call with the draft record.
  2. Tick every field that is incomplete or ambiguous.
  3. Write a concise read-back that confirms all essential details without broadcasting private information.

Success criteria

  • Relative date is converted to an exact date
  • Contact detail is not omitted
  • Access request is not reinterpreted
  • Read-back closes the loop

Support

Who, what, when, where, special request, contact, confirmation.

Extension

Explain why 'next Friday' and 'six' can both create preventable handover errors.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
