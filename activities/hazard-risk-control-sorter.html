Task 1 · Activity 2

Hazard, risk or control?

Distinguish a source of harm from its possible consequence and the action used to eliminate or minimise exposure.

20–30 minutesIndividualAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

A café team is reviewing a pre-service inspection. Classify each statement before proposing one stronger control.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Classify all six statements.
  2. Check the result and read the explanation for any mismatch.
  3. Choose one control and explain why it is stronger than only warning people.

Success criteria

  • Hazard and harm are not confused
  • Controls act on the exposure pathway
  • Escalation is used for equipment faults

Support

Hazard = source; risk/harm = what may happen; control = what changes the exposure.

Extension

Add a review question that would test whether one control remains effective during service.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
