Service recovery decision path

Supplied context

The scenario

A customer says: 'We ordered twenty minutes ago and one meal is still missing. No one has told us what is happening.' The learner can investigate and communicate but cannot offer refunds or promises outside the venue's authority.

Method

Steps

  1. Complete the four decision points.
  2. Practise the response in pairs.
  3. Record the fact, action, result and improvement without blaming a person.

Success criteria

  • Concern is acknowledged
  • Facts are checked
  • Authority boundary is respected
  • Customer and record receive closure

Support

Listen → clarify → investigate → act/escalate → confirm → record.

Extension

Suggest one system improvement that could prevent the same communication gap.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
