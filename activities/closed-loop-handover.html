Supplied context

The scenario

Handover stimulus: Table 6 is waiting for two hot drinks. One customer requested a non-dairy option; the authorised product has been confirmed and the cups are ready. The espresso machine has just shown an unfamiliar warning. No drink has been started. The supervisor needs to be told before equipment use continues.

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Build a concise handover from the stimulus.
  2. Deliver it to a partner.
  3. Receiver repeats the order, constraint and next action.
  4. Observer checks the nine fields and records one improvement.

Success criteria

  • Facts are complete and concise
  • Warning is not diagnosed by guesswork
  • Authority/escalation is clear
  • Read-back and closure occur

Support

Use: situation, task, constraint/risk, action, owner, confirmation.

Extension

Rewrite the handover for a written docket note while protecting customer privacy.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
