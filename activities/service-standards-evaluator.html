Supplied context

The scenario

Standard: acknowledge promptly, listen without interruption, confirm the request, record accurately, protect privacy and close the interaction with a clear next step.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Classify each observation.
  2. Check your judgement.
  3. Rewrite one 'needs repair' behaviour as an observable service action.

Success criteria

  • Judgements use observable behaviour
  • Accuracy and privacy are included
  • Repair statement is specific

Support

Avoid personality labels. Describe what the worker did or failed to do.

Extension

Add one measurable indicator for the end of an interaction.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
