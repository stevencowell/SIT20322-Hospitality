Beverage and serviceware match

Select clean, suitable serviceware and handling points for common non-alcoholic beverage categories.

Supplied context

The scenario

The venue serves water, chilled juice, tea and teacher-directed espresso beverages. Exact sizes and local presentation standards come from the authorised service procedure.

Method

Steps

  1. Match four service needs.
  2. Check the handling and suitability explanation.
  3. Write a five-point pre-service serviceware check.

Success criteria

  • Item is suitable for temperature/product
  • Food-contact areas are protected
  • Damage is identified
  • Local size/presentation is not invented

Support

Check clean, sound, suitable, available, handled correctly.

Extension

Explain how glassware or cup choice affects both safety and perceived quality.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
