Task 4 · Activity 20

Coffee quality diagnosis

Use observable evidence to decide whether to serve, remake, check the recipe or escalate an equipment issue.

30 minutesPairsAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

Work only from observable product and workflow evidence. Do not change machine settings unless the teacher has authorised that adjustment.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

The cup is chipped before production.

The finished drink does not match the recorded order.

Repeated products are outside the demonstrated quality standard.

A machine warning or fault appears.

Method

Steps

  1. Choose the response for each observation.
  2. Read the feedback.
  3. Write one quality log entry: order, observation, action, result, person notified.

Success criteria

  • Unacceptable product is not served
  • Recipe is checked before adjustment
  • Faults are escalated
  • Record states observed facts

Support

Observe → compare → protect customer/product → act within authority → record.

Extension

Separate one likely product issue from one likely workflow issue and name the evidence needed to tell them apart.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
