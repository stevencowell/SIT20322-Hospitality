Task 2 · Activity 13

Inclusive service decisions

Adapt communication respectfully while keeping the customer in control of the interaction.

25 minutesSmall groupsAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

Use the customer's words and preferred communication method. A respectful adaptation supports access; it does not diagnose, stereotype or lower the service standard.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

A customer asks you to write down the choices.

A customer has an accent you find unfamiliar.

A customer uses a wheelchair.

A customer asks a religious or cultural dietary question you cannot answer.

Method

Steps

  1. Choose an action for each case.
  2. Read the feedback.
  3. Write one clarification phrase that protects dignity and accuracy.

Success criteria

  • Customer remains central
  • No assumptions are made
  • Uncertain information is checked
  • Adaptation is practical and respectful

Support

Ask what would help, listen, adapt, confirm.

Extension

Identify how a busy service might create barriers and one system change that reduces them for everyone.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
