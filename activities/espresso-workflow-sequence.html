Task 4 · Activity 19

Espresso workflow sequence

Organise a clean, communication-rich espresso service without prescribing local machine settings.

25–35 minutesIndividualAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

A teacher has demonstrated the authorised machine and recipe. Rebuild the general workflow; exact dose, grind, extraction, milk and cleaning settings stay with that demonstration and procedure.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

  1. 1Complete machine and grinder readiness checks as demonstrated
  2. 2Prepare the coffee component using the authorised recipe
  3. 3Prepare any milk component using the authorised technique
  4. 4Assemble and check the product against the service standard
  5. 5Serve promptly with correct identification
  6. 6Reset, clean and communicate stock or equipment issues
  7. 7Read and clarify the beverage order
  8. 8Prepare clean serviceware and the work zone

Method

Steps

  1. Order the eight stages.
  2. Check the workflow.
  3. Mark where order clarification, quality check and handover occur.

Success criteria

  • Order controls the product
  • Readiness precedes production
  • Quality is checked before service
  • Reset and issue communication are included

Support

Order → prepare → produce → check → serve → reset.

Extension

Identify two stages that can overlap in a team and the communication needed to prevent duplication.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
