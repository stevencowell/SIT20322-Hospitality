Task 2 · Activity 10

Customer profiles and needs

Match a customer's stated need to a respectful clarifying question and a suitable service response.

30 minutesSmall groupsAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

The venue is a fictional daytime café with step-free entry, dine-in and takeaway service, a simple menu and staff who can check ingredient information. Do not assume a need that the customer has not expressed.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Customer says: 'I have ten minutes before my bus.'

Customer says: 'I need to avoid a particular ingredient.'

Customer uses a communication aid and asks for more time.

Family asks for a table with room for a mobility device.

Method

Steps

  1. Choose the best response for each stated need.
  2. Check the explanations.
  3. Write one open question and one closed confirmation question for a selected profile.

Success criteria

  • Stated need guides response
  • No medical or cultural assumption is made
  • Information is checked
  • Questions are respectful and useful

Support

Clarify first; then match the available service to the confirmed need.

Extension

Explain how time, price, accessibility and dietary information can interact without becoming the same need.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
