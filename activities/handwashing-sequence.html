Task 1 · Activity 4

Rebuild the handwashing control

Order a complete handwashing process and connect each step to contamination control.

15–20 minutesIndividualAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

A learner has moved from handling raw food to a ready-to-eat preparation zone. The handwashing steps have been mixed up.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

  1. 1Apply soap
  2. 2Clean all hand surfaces for the required time
  3. 3Rinse completely
  4. 4Dry thoroughly using the approved single-use method
  5. 5Avoid recontaminating clean hands before returning to the ready-to-eat zone
  6. 6Move to the designated handwashing basin
  7. 7Wet hands using the authorised water setting

Method

Steps

  1. Move the seven steps into a defensible order.
  2. Check the sequence.
  3. Explain which step is defeated if clean hands touch a contaminated tap, apron or phone.

Success criteria

  • Designated basin used
  • Cleaning occurs before rinsing
  • Drying is complete
  • Recontamination is controlled

Support

Think location → wet → soap → clean → rinse → dry → protect.

Extension

Identify four moments during a sandwich practical when the process must be repeated.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
