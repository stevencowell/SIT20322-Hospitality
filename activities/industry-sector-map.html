Hospitality sector relationship map

Connect hospitality settings, their primary functions and the suppliers or related sectors that support them.

30 minutesSmall groupsAutosaves locallyPrint-ready

Supplied context

The scenario

Fictional regional event: a hotel hosts a two-day conference with accommodation, breakfast, meeting rooms and an evening function. External transport, local producers and an audio-visual supplier are also involved.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Hotel front office

Food and beverage team

Local producer

Audio-visual supplier

Method

Steps

  1. Match each organisation to its function.
  2. Draw four information or product flows between them.
  3. Mark one point where an inaccurate handover could affect the guest.

Success criteria

  • Functions are distinguished
  • Related industries are included
  • Information and product flows are visible
  • Guest impact is explained

Support

Ask what each group supplies, receives and must communicate.

Extension

Add one quality-assurance or compliance information source used by a selected team.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
