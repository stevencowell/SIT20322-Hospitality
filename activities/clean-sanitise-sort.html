Clean, sanitise or unsafe?

Distinguish soil removal, microorganism reduction and unsafe chemical shortcuts.

20–25 minutesIndividualAutosaves locallyPrint-ready

Supplied context

The scenario

A closing team has recorded six actions. Classify what each action actually achieves.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Classify all actions.
  2. Check the result.
  3. Write the source you must use for product, dilution, PPE, contact time and rinse directions.

Success criteria

  • Cleaning precedes sanitising
  • Visible soil is not ignored
  • Chemical directions come from authorised sources
  • Products are never mixed by guesswork

Support

Cleaning removes soil; sanitising follows effective cleaning; unsafe actions bypass the authorised source.

Extension

Draft a verification question for a food-contact surface after the sequence is complete.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
