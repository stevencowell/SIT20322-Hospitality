Supplied context

The scenario

Worked example topic: digital ordering. Possible benefits include order visibility and reduced re-entry; possible risks include accessibility barriers, privacy concerns, system outages and poor handover between online and in-person work.

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Method

Steps

  1. Use the worked example or a teacher-approved current topic.
  2. Complete all eight brief fields.
  3. Separate verified evidence from your inference.
  4. Prepare a one-minute colleague update.

Success criteria

  • Two credible sources are used
  • Claim and inference are separated
  • Workplace effect is concrete
  • Recommendation includes a check/review point

Support

Use: evidence says…; this may mean…; before acting we must check…

Extension

Add one person who should be consulted and explain why their experience changes the recommendation.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
