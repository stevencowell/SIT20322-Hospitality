Sandwich production and waste sequence

Build a complete product workflow from a supplied neutral brief.

25–35 minutesIndividualAutosaves locallyPrint-ready
Return to linked lesson

Supplied context

The scenario

Brief: prepare four consistent ready-to-eat salad sandwiches using the teacher-authorised recipe. The exact ingredients, quantities and local presentation standard remain in that recipe.

Materials

  • This page and the supplied scenario
  • Your saved learning record

Interactive practice

Complete the job

  1. 1Gather and check ingredients and equipment
  2. 2Set separated zones, waste controls and handwashing points
  3. 3Prepare ingredients in the safe authorised sequence
  4. 4Portion and assemble consistently
  5. 5Check presentation and protect for service or storage
  6. 6Record usable surplus and dispose of unavoidable waste correctly
  7. 7Clean, sanitise as required and complete an honest reflection
  8. 8Confirm recipe, yield and allergy information
  9. 9Prepare yourself and inspect the work area

Method

Steps

  1. Order all nine stages.
  2. Check the sequence.
  3. Name one waste-prevention decision and one food-safety control that must operate together.

Success criteria

  • Recipe and yield control the task
  • Ready-to-eat separation is visible
  • Quality check occurs before service
  • Waste and clean-down are part of the job

Support

Plan → prepare → produce → protect → review.

Extension

Identify the two stages most likely to create rework if skipped and justify your choice.

Set-up

The stimulus and core materials are supplied. Substitute a local authorised source only when it materially improves relevance and is safe to share.

Observe

Listen for use of evidence, accurate language, authority boundaries and the learner's explanation—not just the final selection.

Debrief

Ask one group to defend a decision, one group to identify a likely error and one group to state the next source or person to confirm with.
