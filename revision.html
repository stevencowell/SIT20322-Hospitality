Four-pass revision routine

  1. RecallAnswer a lesson check without notes.
  2. ExplainRead the feedback and state why the distractors fail.
  3. ApplyUse the idea in a short workplace scenario or activity.
  4. ImproveWrite one specific gap and link the lesson that repairs it.
